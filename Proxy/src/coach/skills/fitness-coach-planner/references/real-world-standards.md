# Real-World Standards

Use these as external comparison anchors, not as the app's consistency score definitions.

## Marine Corps PFT Anchors

- The Marine Corps PFT pull-up perfect-score range varies by age and sex.
- Current published pull-up maximum-score table values range roughly from 20-23 for men and 4-12 for women across age groups.
- The Marine Corps plank perfect-score standard is 3:45.
- Push-ups are an alternative scoring event in the Marine Corps table, but they are not equivalent to a 100-point pull-up score in all contexts.

Sources:

- USMC pull-up / push-up scoring table: https://www.fitness.marines.mil/Portals/211/Docs/PFT_CFT/PFT_CFT%20Standards/Table%202-2%20Hybrid%20Pull-up%20Push-up%20Test%20Scoring%20Tables.pdf?ver=2019-01-04-154009-413
- USMC plank scoring table: https://www.fitness.marines.mil/Portals/211/Docs/PFT_CFT/PFT_CFT%20Standards/Plank%20Scoring%20Table.pdf?ver=qjyQlKiDx7i5hVOH6DCfkw%3D%3D

## General Health Anchors

- CDC guidance based on the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans recommends muscle-strengthening activity at least 2 days per week for adults.
- CDC notes that gradually increasing weight and repetitions in strengthening activities can provide more benefit.

Sources:

- CDC adult activity basics: https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/adding-adults/index.html
- CDC benefits of physical activity: https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/benefits/

## Strength-Standard Anchors

ExRx strength standards can be used as a broad comparison reference, but bodyweight standards depend on age, sex, body weight, and strict execution.

Source:

- ExRx strength standards: https://exrx.net/WorkoutTools/StrengthStandards

## App Interpretation

The app's consistency score is artificial and should be labeled as an app-specific adherence signal.
Do not present consistency scores as official military, sport, or medical classifications.

When comparing goals:

- 50 strict pull-ups is far above common military max-score standards.
- 5:00 plank is above the USMC current perfect-score plank duration.
- 100 strict push-ups is a high endurance target, but comparisons depend heavily on form standard and time limit.
